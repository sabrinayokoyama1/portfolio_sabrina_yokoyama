About Me

I am a Data Science and Artificial Intelligence undergraduate student at the University of Sorocaba and currently work as a Data Assistant at Banco Afinz, where I have been contributing for over three years. I see data as a powerful tool to generate impact, uncover opportunities, and build purpose-driven solutions.
